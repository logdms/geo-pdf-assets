02 / Findings

What's broken. Four things.

Critical · Fix Immediately

Zero Schema.org markup sitewide.

What happened
No JSON-LD, no microdata on any of the 13 pages. Open Graph is present; Schema.org is not.
Why it matters
AI systems can't read a structured card for ADM, so they guess from surrounding prose. Every other Fortune 100 ships this.
How to fix it
Inject Corporation, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, NewsArticle, Person via Cloudflare Worker, no CMS release needed.
Category score: 5/100 · 10% of composite
Audit · Dead Link

/en-us/insights/ returns 404.

What happened
The homepage nav points to an Insights hub that doesn't resolve. A near-identical URL does.
Why it matters
Insights articles drive most of ADM's AI citation surface. Every day it's broken, Cargill / Bunge / IFF take the queries.
How to fix it
One 301 at the edge: /en-us/insights/ → /en-us/insights-and-innovation/. Under an hour.
Affects: homepage nav · sitemap · crawl graph
Audit · Governance

No standing page for the 2024 restatement.

What happened
The Jan 2026 closure release is well-written, but buried in the newsroom. No consolidated governance hub exists.
Why it matters
AI answers on "how did ADM respond?" get assembled from Fortune and Bloomberg, not from ADM itself.
How to fix it
Launch /about-adm/governance/: timeline, outcome, remediation, restated 10-K. Article schema + dates. Link from footer.
Observed: 1 news item · 0 standing pages
Audit · Homepage

The hero has no citable facts.

What happened
H1 "Pioneering Innovation from Farmer to Customer", no numbers, no dates, no entities.
Why it matters
Homepage is the heaviest-weighted AI surface. The citable line, $85B · 44,000 · six continents, exists, just on the About page.
How to fix it
Lead the hero with a quantified "ADM is…" sentence. Slogan drops to a supporting role.
Citability: 38/100 · homepage 42 vs About 58
Emerging Standard

No AGENTS.md. No llms.txt.

What they are
Two plain Markdown files at the site root. llms.txt points AI at the authoritative pages. AGENTS.md tells autonomous agents how to interact, what's canonical, what's licensed, who to contact.
Why now
Cloudflare, OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Sourcegraph all support the convention. Tens of thousands of sites already publish them.
How to fix it
Serve /llms.txt, /llms-full.txt, /AGENTS.md from a Cloudflare Worker. ~1 day combined.
Infrastructure · Already In Place

adm.com already runs on Cloudflare.

What this unlocks
Most fixes in this audit can ship at the edge, no AEM release, no content-team dependency, same dashboard already in use.
Cloudflare features that do the work
Workers, JSON-LD injection, /llms.txt, /AGENTS.md, 301s. ~150 lines of JS.
AI Audit · AI Labyrinth, explicit GPTBot / ClaudeBot / PerplexityBot / Google-Extended policy.
